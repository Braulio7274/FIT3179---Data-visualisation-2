01

Where do Australians come from?

Australia is home to people from a wide range of countries and linguistic backgrounds. These visualisations show some of the largest communities represented within the population.

02

Where do culturally diverse communities live?

Cultural diversity is not distributed evenly across Australia. These maps show both the share and number of overseas-born residents across different areas.

03

How do states differ?

Comparing languages and measures of cultural diversity reveals important differences between Australian states and territories.

04

How does migration connect Australia to the world?

Major migration flows connect Australia with countries around the world, with different origins contributing to different destination states.

05

How do migration streams distribute across Australia?

Skilled, family and humanitarian migration streams contribute differently to Australia's states and territories.

Data sources: Australian Bureau of Statistics and Australian Government Department of Home Affairs.
