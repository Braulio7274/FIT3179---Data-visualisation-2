UNDERSTANDING THE DATA

Exploring Australia's cultural diversity through two data sources

This visualisation combines data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) and the Australian Government Department of Home Affairs.

ABS data is used to examine country of birth, languages spoken and where culturally diverse communities are located across Australia.

Department of Home Affairs settlement data is used to explore migration streams and where new settlers established themselves between 2016 and 2025.

Important: The two datasets represent different populations and periods. ABS Census data describes Australia's resident population, while Home Affairs data records settlement activity. Their values should therefore not always be compared directly.

Where do Australians come from?

Australia is home to people from a wide range of countries and linguistic backgrounds. These visualisations highlight some of the largest communities represented in the population.

Where do culturally diverse communities live?

Cultural diversity is not distributed evenly across Australia. These maps show how overseas-born populations vary between different areas.

How do states differ?

Comparing language communities and broader measures of diversity reveals differences between Australian states and territories.

04

How does migration connect Australia to the world?

Migration connects Australia with countries across the world, creating major flows between origin countries and destinations within Australia.
