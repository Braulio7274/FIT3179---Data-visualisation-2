AUSTRALIAN CULTURAL DIVERSITY

Cultural Diversity in Australia

Exploring where Australians come from, where culturally diverse communities live, how states differ, and how migration continues to shape Australia.

01

Where do Australians come from?

Australia is home to people from a wide range of countries and linguistic backgrounds. These visualisations show some of the largest communities represented within the population.

02

Where do culturally diverse communities live?

Cultural diversity is not distributed evenly across Australia. Some areas have considerably larger overseas-born populations than others.

03

How do states differ?

Comparing languages and diversity across states reveals differences in the cultural composition of Australia's population.

04

How does migration connect Australia to the world?

Migration links Australia with countries across the world and contributes differently to each state and territory.

05

How do migration streams distribute across Australia?

Skilled, family and humanitarian migration streams contribute differently to Australia's states and territories.

Data sources: Australian Bureau of Statistics and Australian Government Department of Home Affairs.
