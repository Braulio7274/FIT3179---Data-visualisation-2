UNDERSTANDING THE DATA

Two datasets tell different parts of Australia's story

This visualisation combines data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) with settlement data from the Australian Government Department of Home Affairs.

ABS data is used to explore country of birth, languages spoken and the geographic distribution of culturally diverse communities. Home Affairs data is used to examine migration streams and where new settlers have established themselves across Australia.
